XRP-Quoted B-Book Perpetuals: Feasibility and Design

Feasible, medium risk. Matching, settlement, funding transfers, liquidation and ADL already work per quote asset and need no rewrite. Five things must be built: a synthetic cross price, a funding rate, XRP transfers into the perp account, per-asset margin maths, and USD conversion for downstream services.

What this document is

The answer to "investigate the possibility and limitations of adding XRP B-book markets to the perpetuals (btc/xrp, eth/xrp)". Every statement about the current system was checked in the neodax code on 2026-11-18 and carries a file reference. Items that could not be confirmed are listed in chapter 08. This is a proposal: nothing has been implemented.

Verdict by area

Main risks

Synthetic price errorsThe mark price depends on two feeds. If either leg is stale or wrong, users can be liquidated at a wrong price. Mitigated by leg-freshness rules and safe-mode inheritance (chapter 05).
Bankruptcy losses in XRPThe insurance pool starts at 0, so every liquidation shortfall lands on the settlement pool as a negative XRP balance. Its USD value grows when XRP rises.
Liquidity depends on one market makerThese markets can never move to A-Book. If the internal MM stops quoting, the market has to go close-only.
Higher volatilityA cross pair moves with both BTC and XRP. Leverage must be lower and maintenance margin higher than on the USDT markets.

Recommended first release

Two markets (BTCXRP-PERP, ETHXRP-PERP), isolated margin only, maximum leverage 10x, an open-interest cap per market, and a soft launch visible only to the market maker. Cross margin for XRP markets follows in a second phase.

Estimated backend effort: 4 to 6 person-weeks, excluding frontend and the market maker's own work.

Product Definition

The request can be read in two ways. This document follows Definition A, as confirmed by the requester.

A. XRP-quoted, XRP-margined (chosen)B. USDT market, XRP as collateral
PriceXRP per 1 BTCUSDT per 1 BTC
Margin, PnL, feesXRPUSDT, with XRP valued at a haircut
What the user holdsLong BTC and short XRP at the same timeLong BTC only
Engine changeSmall: reuses "quote asset is the collateral"Large: haircuts and collateral liquidation
External liquidity and hedgeNone exists; synthetic onlyUnchanged

Confirmed assumptions

  • Markets are XRP-quoted and XRP-margined. PnL and fees are paid in XRP.
  • Liquidity and hedging are provided by our own market maker. The MM is an ordinary perp account that rests orders through trading-api and hedges outside this repository.
  • The insurance pool starts with 0 XRP. Losses are accepted on the pool accounts.
  • The design follows the practice of the largest centralised exchanges where one applies.

What users should understand

A long BTCXRP position gains when BTC rises against XRP. The gain is paid in XRP, so its USD value also depends on the XRP price. A trader who is right on BTC/XRP can still see a smaller USD result if XRP falls against the dollar. The UI should show both the XRP amount and an indicative USD value.

Why most exchanges do not list this

A check of public instrument lists on 2026-11-18 found no BTC/XRP or ETH/XRP perpetual, and no XRP-quoted market of any kind, on Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken, Coinbase, Bitget, Gate or Deribit (details in 05b). A market maker has to hedge with two legs (BTCUSDT and XRPUSDT), the hedge earns USDT while the liability is XRP, and demand is spread thin. The design below accepts these costs because we have a committed internal market maker.

Closest precedent: Binance ETHBTC perpetual

Binance lists one perpetual with a non-stablecoin quote: ETHBTC, quoted and margined in BTC, live since May 2023. It is the same shape as Definition A, and its index and funding setup is the reference for this design.

Design 1 of 4

Margin Model: Separate by Quote Asset

Follow the model the large exchanges use for USDT-margined and coin-margined futures: each collateral asset is its own margin group. XRP margin never supports a USDT position, and the reverse.

Changes

  1. Group all margin maths by quote assetUnrealized PnL, maintenance margin and equity are computed per quote asset. Affected: GetAllBalances, distributeReusableUnrealizedPnL and GetCrossMarginAccountEquityAtWorstCasePrices in margin_manager.go, and the inputs of calculator/cross.go. The grouping helper domain.SumAllocatedMarginByQuoteAsset already exists and shows the pattern.
  2. Isolated-only switch per marketAdd an isolated_only column to perps_markets. The rule already exists for A-Book markets (lock_prepare.go:133-136, margin_mode.go:33-39, margin_mode_cache.go:178-186); generalise it from "A-Book" to "A-Book or isolated_only". XRP markets launch with it set.
  3. Validate the order assetPM Perp should reject a lock request whose asset is not the market's quote asset. Today it trusts trading-api.
  4. Account totalsREST, WebSocket and Kafka account totals are either converted to USD per asset before summing, or returned per asset. The *_usd fields are already converted per asset and stay as they are.

Why step 1 is needed even with isolated-only markets

The isolated order pre-check and the withdrawal cap read the per-asset available balance, and that balance currently includes PnL from positions in other quote assets. A USDT profit would inflate the XRP available balance at the wrong scale.

Phase 2: cross margin for XRP markets

Cross margin needs the liquidation path to run per asset group: the scanner, stage 0 and stage 1, and LiquidationCompleteOp, which carries a single asset today (raft/types/change.go:229-234). The account lock is account-wide and would also need to be scoped per group. This is a separate project and is not needed for the first release.

Design 2 of 4

Synthetic Price and Funding Rate

Both are derived from the two USDT perpetuals we already price: BTCUSDT-PERP and XRPUSDT-PERP (ETHUSDT-PERP for the ETH market).

Price

# derived on every update of either leg
index(BTCXRP) = index(BTCUSDT) / index(XRPUSDT)
mark(BTCXRP)  = mark(BTCUSDT)  / mark(XRPUSDT)

Add a "derived market" stage to the quote service, placed after the existing publish point in quotes_publisher.go. It caches the latest output of each leg, recomputes when either changes, and publishes a normal EventQuoteObserved for the derived symbol. Because it consumes our own aggregated output, it works on both the legacy path and the multi-venue oracle path, and it inherits the outlier filtering of each leg.

Protection rules

  • NO PRICEThe two legs' timestamps differ by more than a threshold (suggested 1 to 2 seconds).
  • SAFEEither leg is in SAFE_MODE: the derived market is in SAFE_MODE.
  • QUALITYMark quality is the worse of the two legs. AllowOpen is true only if both legs allow opening.
  • PHASE 1Internal trades (internal_perp_trade) do not feed the mark, so a thin book cannot move it.
  • CHECKCompare the synthetic price with the inverse of the XRPBTC (or XRPETH) spot mid on Binance. A sustained gap above a threshold (suggested 1%) raises an alert and blocks new opens. The spot pair is a cross-check only; it does not feed the price (see 05b).

Also fix parseSymbolToMarket to read the market configuration instead of a list of quote suffixes.

Funding rate

# long BTCXRP pays when positive
funding(BTCXRP) = funding(BTCUSDT) - funding(XRPUSDT)

The derived stage computes the rate and sends it in the same EventQuoteObserved. PM Perp's intake, validation and settlement stay unchanged.

Why the difference of the two legs

The market maker who is short BTCXRP hedges with long BTCUSDT and short XRPUSDT. On that hedge they pay the BTC funding and receive the XRP funding. Charging the difference on our market passes exactly that carry cost through, so the market maker is not exposed to funding arbitrage. Funding on a B-Book market moves between position holders; no house account is involved (funding_rate/executor.go:206-228).

Binance prices funding on its ETHBTC perpetual with the standard premium-index formula and an interest rate of 0. That formula needs a stable internal order book, so it should be evaluated in phase 2, once the book has depth.

Details to handle

  • If the two legs have different funding intervals, normalise both to the derived market's interval before subtracting.
  • Set max_funding_rate_per_int for the difference; it can be larger than either leg's cap.
  • Add a freshness check to the in-memory funding snapshot. Today a market whose feed stops keeps settling with its last rate (funding_rate/types.go:73-82).

Design 4 of 4

Risk Controls and Launch

The market maker carries the directional risk. The platform's remaining exposure is liquidation shortfalls, which land on the settlement pool in XRP.

Where a loss goes

  1. User marginAn isolated position can lose at most its allocated margin. User accounts cannot go negative from a trade (margin_manager/settlement.go:243-275).
  2. Insurance pool (XRP)Starts at 0 and is clamped at 0. It grows only from liquidation fees and surplus.
  3. Settlement pool (XRP)Absorbs whatever the insurance pool cannot cover and may go negative ("unfunded absorbed by settlement", raft/fsm/balance.go:1008-1038). This is the account that records the accepted loss.

The loss is in XRP

A negative XRP balance is a liability whose USD value rises when XRP rises. Treasury should decide whether to cover it periodically or let it run.

New controls

Open-interest cap per marketExpressed in the quote asset. When reached, only reducing orders are accepted. B-Book has no such limit today.
Settlement-pool alert and auto close-onlyAlert when the XRP balance turns negative. Past a threshold, switch the market to close-only through the existing operational_state.
Conservative parametersMaximum leverage 10x. Maintenance margin above the USDT market of the same base asset.
Parameters in XRP unitsRisk tiers, min_notional, tick size and precision are set per market. The default min_notional=1 would mean 1 XRP.

Launch sequence

  1. Create the markets as INVISIBLEOnly sessions in market_maker_sessions can see and trade them.
  2. Market maker funds and quotesThe MM account transfers XRP in and rests two-sided orders.
  3. DrillsRun one funding settlement and one forced isolated liquidation with test accounts. Check the pool balances and alerts.
  4. Open to usersOne market first. Open the second after an observation window.

Limitations and Open Questions

What the first release will not do, what it depends on, and what still needs an answer.

Limitations

  • NEVERThese markets cannot move to A-Book. No external venue lists the contracts (checked 2026-11-18), and mm_connector requires a Binance symbol.
  • MMLiquidity depends entirely on the internal market maker. If it stops, the market goes close-only.
  • PHASE 1No cross margin. XRP collateral cannot offset USDT positions, and the reverse.
  • LOSSBankruptcy losses are in XRP and land on the settlement pool, because the insurance pool starts at 0.
  • DEPENDSXRPUSDT-PERP must stay live: the synthetic price, the funding rate and USD conversion all use it.
  • DEPENDSAn XRPUSDT spot market must be listed: referral conversion and analytics pricing use it.

Open questions

  • Hyperliquid and smaller venues were not checked for BTC/XRP or ETH/XRP perpetuals.
