XRP-Quoted B-Book Perpetuals: Feasibility and Design

Feasible, medium risk. Matching, settlement and funding transfers already work per quote asset. The account side, meaning margin and liquidation grouped by settlement asset, XRP transfers, the account-summary API and pool routing, follows the multiple-settlement-assets design (Perpetuals: Multiple Settlement Assets, status Proposed) (chapter 04). This proposal covers only what that design does not: the synthetic index and mark price, the funding rate, USD reporting values, B-Book risk caps and the launch configuration.

What this document is

The answer to "investigate the possibility and limitations of adding XRP B-book markets to the perpetuals (btc/xrp, eth/xrp)". Main risks

Wrong synthetic priceThe mark depends on two feeds. If either leg is stale or wrong, users can be liquidated at a bad price. Mitigated by leg freshness rules and SAFE_MODE inheritance (05, 05c). In production both legs use only the Binance mark, so a Binance outage hits both legs at once.
Bad debt is in XRPThe XRP insurance pool starts at 0 (a confirmed assumption, chapter 02), so every liquidation shortfall lands on the settlement pool as a negative XRP balance. Its USD value grows when XRP rises.
Liquidity depends on one market makerThese markets can never move to A-Book. If the internal MM stops quoting, the market can only go reduce-only.
Higher volatilityA cross pair moves with both BTC and XRP. Leverage must be lower and maintenance margin higher than on USDT markets.

Delivery estimate (TODO to confirm): development is done by AI, so the cost is human review and verification. This proposal's 10 small PRs plus review take about 2 to 3 weeks and can run in parallel with the multiple-settlement-assets design. Launch depends on when that design's release gates (§12) pass, plus about 1 week of drills. Front-end and market-maker work are not included.

Proposed phase 1 scope

Two markets (BTCXRP-PERP, ETHXRP-PERP), isolated and cross margin (cross grouped by settlement asset, see 04), 10x max leverage, an open-interest cap per market, and a soft launch visible only to the market maker.

02Product Definition

The request can be read in two ways. This document follows Definition A, as confirmed by the requester.

A. XRP-quoted, XRP-margined (chosen)B. USDT market, XRP as collateral
PriceXRP per 1 BTCUSDT per 1 BTC
Margin, PnL, feesXRPUSDT, with XRP valued at a haircut
What the user holdsLong BTC and short XRP at the same timeLong BTC only
Engine changeSmall: reuses "quote asset is the collateral"Large: haircuts and collateral liquidation
External liquidity and hedgeNone exists; synthetic onlyUnchanged

Confirmed assumptions

  • Markets are XRP-quoted and XRP-margined. PnL and fees are paid in XRP.
  • Liquidity and hedging are provided by our own market maker. The MM is an ordinary perp account that rests orders through trading-api and hedges outside this repository.
  • The insurance pool starts with 0 XRP. Losses are accepted on the pool accounts.
  • The design follows the practice of the largest centralised exchanges where one applies.

What users should understand

A long BTCXRP position gains when BTC rises against XRP. The gain is paid in XRP, so its USD value also depends on the XRP price. A trader who is right on BTC/XRP can still see a smaller USD result if XRP falls against the dollar. The UI should show both the XRP amount and an indicative USD value.

Why most exchanges do not list this

A check of public instrument lists on 2026-11-18 found no BTC/XRP or ETH/XRP perpetual, and no XRP-quoted market of any kind, on Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken, Coinbase, Bitget, Gate or Deribit (details in 05b). A market maker has to hedge with two legs (BTCUSDT and XRPUSDT), the hedge earns USDT while the liability is XRP, and demand is spread thin. The design below accepts these costs because we have a committed internal market maker.

Closest precedent: Binance ETHBTC perpetual

Binance lists one perpetual with a non-stablecoin quote: ETHBTC, quoted and margined in BTC, live since May 2023. It is the same shape as Definition A, and its index and funding setup is the reference for this design.

Design 2 of 4

05Synthetic Price and Funding RateUPDATED v6

Both are derived from the two USDT perpetuals we already price: BTCUSDT-PERP and XRPUSDT-PERP (ETHUSDT-PERP for the ETH market).

Price

# derived on every update of either leg
index(BTCXRP) = index(BTCUSDT) / index(XRPUSDT)
mark(BTCXRP)  = mark(BTCUSDT)  / mark(XRPUSDT)

Add a "derived market" stage to the quote service, placed after the existing publish point in quotes_publisher.go. It caches the latest output of each leg, recomputes when either changes, and publishes a normal EventQuoteObserved for the derived symbol. Because it consumes our own aggregated output, it works on both the legacy path and the multi-venue oracle path, and it inherits the outlier filtering of each leg.

Protection rules

  • NO PRICEThe two legs' timestamps differ by more than a threshold (suggested 1 to 2 seconds).
  • SAFEEither leg is in SAFE_MODE: the derived market is in SAFE_MODE.
  • QUALITYMark quality is the worse of the two legs. AllowOpen is true only if both legs allow opening.
  • PHASE 1Internal trades (internal_perp_trade) do not feed the mark, so a thin book cannot move it.
  • CHECKCompare the synthetic price with the inverse of the XRPBTC (or XRPETH) spot mid on Binance. A sustained gap above a threshold (suggested 1%) raises an alert and blocks new opens. The spot pair is a cross-check only; it does not feed the price (see 05b).

Also fix parseSymbolToMarket to read the market configuration instead of a list of quote suffixes.

Funding rate

# long BTCXRP pays when positive
funding(BTCXRP) = funding(BTCUSDT) - funding(XRPUSDT)

The derived stage computes the rate and sends it in the same EventQuoteObserved. PM Perp's intake, validation and settlement stay unchanged.

Binance prices funding on its ETHBTC perpetual with the standard premium-index formula and an interest rate of 0. That formula needs a stable internal order book, so it should be evaluated in phase 2, once the book has depth.

Details to handle

  • If the two legs have different funding intervals, normalise both to the derived market's interval before subtracting.
  • Set max_funding_rate_per_int for the difference; it can be larger than either leg's cap.
  • Add a freshness check to the in-memory funding snapshot. Today a market whose feed stops keeps settling with its last rate (funding_rate/types.go:73-82).
Why the difference of the two legs

The market maker who is short BTCXRP hedges with long BTCUSDT and short XRPUSDT. On that hedge they pay the BTC funding and receive the XRP funding. Charging the difference on our market passes exactly that carry cost through, so the market maker is not exposed to funding arbitrage. Funding on a B-Book market moves between position holders; no house account is involved (funding_rate/executor.go:206-228).

3. Hedge ratio: how the market maker replicates the contract

Long q BTCXRP equals opposite positions of equal notional on the two USDT legs, not equal coin amounts. Below, B = mark(BTCUSDT), X = mark(XRPUSDT), S = B / X.

LegSideAmountUSDT notional
BTCUSDTLongq BTCq × B
XRPUSDTShortq × S XRPq × S × X = q × B

Short BTCXRP reverses both legs.

# prices move from (B0, X0) to (B1, X1)
hedge_pnl_USDT = q × (B1 − B0) − q × S0 × (X1 − X0)
               = q × X1 × (S1 − S0)
contract_pnl_XRP = q × (S1 − S0)      # equals the line above once converted at X1

A hedge built at the current S is exact at that moment. The currencies differ, though: the hedge PnL lands in USDT while the market maker's PnL on our market is in XRP. After prices move, the XRP short must be brought back to q × S, or the USDT PnL converted to XRP; otherwise the accumulated PnL is exposed to XRP/USDT. The funding difference in 05a-4 is exact only at this ratio.

  • The rebalancing threshold (how far S may drift before an adjustment) is the market maker's call (TODO). Hedging is outside this repo; we only state the requirement in the market-maker integration notes.
  • Residual risk: in production our leg marks also come from Binance (05c), so the gap to the market maker's hedge comes mainly from the timing of the aggregation window. The market maker bears it.
  • Fees and funding are paid in XRP; the market maker decides whether to convert back to USDT or hold XRP inventory.
v5 change #8The external proposal review (2026-11-25) raised equal-notional hedging of the two legs. This adds the PnL derivation and why rebalancing is needed.

2. Index price

The index is the reference spot-like price of the pair. It is not used for PnL or liquidation (the mark is). It is used for display, for the mark/index deviation guard, and as the base of a future basis term.

Today's source EXISTING

For USDT markets the index is relayed from Binance's own multi-exchange index (IndexPrice, field i in binance_perp/types.go) inside the auxiliary data of EventQuoteObserved. Our weighted mark aggregator does not compute the index.

Rule for the XRP markets NEW

# recomputed whenever either leg's index changes
index(BTCXRP) = index(BTCUSDT) / index(XRPUSDT)
index(ETHXRP) = index(ETHUSDT) / index(XRPUSDT)
# rounded to the market's price precision with the publisher's existing rounding
  • NO UPDATEEither leg index is missing, zero, or older than 2 s (TODO). The previous derived index stays in place. It is never computed from one fresh and one stale leg.
  • STALEThe derived index is older than 10 s (TODO). The market goes to SAFE_MODE (same trigger time as safe_mode_zero_sources_sec).
  • OUTPUTPublished in the same event and topic as every other market's index, so consumers need no change.

Industry practice (section 6):

  • 3 to 6 spot venues feed the index.
  • A venue more than 3% to 5% from the median is capped or dropped (1% for BTC, ETH, XRP on Binance).
  • A venue silent for 3 to 15 minutes is dropped.
  • A synthetic cross is accepted for pairs without a direct market.

Our legs already follow these rules through Binance's own index.

A ratio of two composite indices inherits the outlier filtering of each. Binance's own ETHBTC index uses the same idea: a synthetic cross ETHUSDT/BTCUSDT is one of its constituents (05b).

3. Mark price

The mark drives unrealised PnL, margin, liquidation and the funding notional. It is never the last trade price.

Today's source EXISTING

The aggregator supports per-source weights (the repository default config shows BTCUSDT-PERP at Binance 50%, OKX 30%, Bybit 15%, internal trades 5%). In all three deployed environments, however, every market is weighted binance: 1 (05c), so in practice each USDT market's mark is the Binance mark. The aggregator runs in three modes:

  • NORMAL: enough valid sources, the weighted value is used directly.
  • DEGRADED: with only two sources, a blend 0.7 × raw + 0.3 × anchor.
  • SAFE_MODE: the price is frozen.

A source drops offline after 10 s without data or a 5% deviation. See configs/nacos/quote.yaml.

Rule for the XRP markets NEW, phase 1

mark(BTCXRP) = mark(BTCUSDT) / mark(XRPUSDT)    # each leg is the aggregator's output for that market

Internal trades on the XRP market do not feed the mark in phase 1, so a thin book cannot move it. Each leg's value already carries the 200 ms windowing and the outlier handling above.

Premium relation and what the 2% guard means NEW

P_leg   = mark / index − 1                  # each leg's own premium
P_cross = (1 + P_BTC) / (1 + P_XRP) − 1     # exact

Example (illustrative): BTCUSDT mark 100,100, index 100,000 (+0.10%); XRPUSDT 2.004 and 2.000 (+0.20%). BTCXRP mark 49,950.10, index 50,000, premium −0.0998%. So the 2% guard above means the two leg premiums differ by 2%, which should never happen in normal conditions; if it triggers, a leg's mark or index is wrong. This premium is the mark-to-index gap, not the impact premium index used in funding formulas.

Relation to "use the Binance mark directly"

The external proposal divides the two Binance contract marks directly. Both legs are weighted Binance 1 in production, so our approach gives the same result there. The difference is the implementation: the derived market reads the leg results quote has already computed instead of opening another Binance feed (05c).

  • Same basis as the liquidations of BTCUSDT-PERP and XRPUSDT-PERP: at any moment a user's USDT and XRP positions are valued with the same prices.
  • If operations later add OKX or Bybit weight to a leg, the derived market follows automatically, and it inherits the legs' stale-source removal, DEGRADED, SAFE_MODE and Kafka-outage protection.
  • With a single source in production, a Binance outage puts both legs, and so the derived market, into SAFE_MODE. This is no different from the external proposal.
  • For the index there is no difference: the leg index already comes from Binance.

Phase 2: add a basis term (every major venue has one)

A pure ratio ignores our own order book. Binance, Bybit, Bitget, Gate and Hyperliquid take the median of three:

  1. index × (1 + last funding rate × time to next funding / interval)
  2. index + moving average of (book mid − index)
  3. last trade price

OKX, Deribit and Kraken use index + EMA of (mid − index).

Basis window:

  • Binance, Bitget, Kraken: 30 s
  • Bybit: 2.5 min
  • Gate: 5 min
  • Hyperliquid: 150 s

Phase 2 adopts the median-of-three with a 30 s basis, once the XRP book has stable depth and the market maker's quoting is proven. Until then a thin book must not move the mark.

Worked example (illustrative)

mark(BTCUSDT) = 90,000 and mark(XRPUSDT) = 2.00 give mark(BTCXRP) = 45,000 XRP per BTC. A long of 0.1 BTC opened at 45,000 has notional 4,500 XRP. If the mark rises to 46,000, unrealised PnL = 0.1 × 1,000 = +100 XRP.

4. Funding rate

Rate computation NEW

# every update; T = 28800 s

f_X' = f(XRPUSDT) × T / T_XRPUSDT

Three conditions for the difference to hold NEW

F(BTCXRP) = F(BTCUSDT) − F(XRPUSDT) equals the funding cost of the market maker's hedge only when:

  1. Notionals are equal. Our funding notional is q × mark(BTCXRP) XRP, which converted at mark(XRPUSDT) is q × mark(BTCUSDT) USDT, exactly the BTC leg's notional. The XRP leg must be kept at q × S as in 05-3.
  2. Settlement times match. Our settlement grid is aligned to UTC midnight (NextAlignedSettlementTime in funding_rate/settlement_time.go), so an 8-hour interval settles at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00, the same as the default times of both Binance legs. If Binance moves a leg to 4 hours or 1 hour, the normalisation above keeps the difference equal to the average carry over the interval, but individual settlements no longer match the market maker's one for one.
  3. The legs' actual settled rates are used. Binance streams the predicted rate for the next interval, which keeps changing until settlement. We take the last value before the settlement time (within the 60 s freshness limit above), as the USDT markets already do. It can differ slightly from the rate Binance finally publishes; the market maker bears that.

Settlement EXISTING

payment_XRP = position_amount × mark_at_settlement × rate
rate > 0: long pays, short receives
rate < 0: short pays, long receives
  • Runs at each aligned instant for every open position. Positions locked for liquidation are skipped (executor.go:119-125).
  • On a B-Book market the money moves between position holders; no house account is involved (executor.go:206-228). Longs' total equals shorts' total only if open interest is balanced, which it always is on a matched book.
  • Each payment is recorded as EventPerpetualFundingPaymentRecord in the quote asset.

Phase 2: standard premium-index formula PLAN

Used by Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, Gate, BitMEX, dYdX and Hyperliquid, with small parameter differences (section 6):

P_t = [max(0, impactBid − index) − max(0, index − impactAsk)] / index
      # impact price = price to fill the impact notional on our book
      # impact notional = 200 USDT / initial margin rate, converted to XRP at mark(XRPUSDT)
avgP = weighted average of P_t, sampled every 5 s, weights 1..n (later samples count more)
F    = clamp( avgP + clamp(I − avgP, −0.05%, +0.05%), −cap, +cap )
I    = 0 for a cross market (Binance ETHBTC uses 0); 0.01% per 8 h on USDT markets

It needs a continuous two-sided book, otherwise the impact price is undefined and P falls to 0. It also makes funding pull the book toward the mark. We can run it together with the leg difference (carry component) as F = carry + premium term. The split is a risk decision (TODO).

Dynamic interval

Binance, OKX, Bybit and Gate shorten the interval to 1 h, 2 h or 4 h when the rate hits its cap, and restore it after stable cycles (Binance: 16 cycles at or below 0.025%). Phase 1 fixes the XRP markets at 8 h. If a leg switches interval, the normalisation above keeps the difference correct. Dynamic intervals are phase 2.

What funding does not do here

The rate has no premium component, so funding does not pull the book price toward the mark. Convergence comes from the market maker's hedge. If the book trades away from the mark, only arbitrage by the maker closes the gap. Phase 2 may add the premium-index term.

Worked example (illustrative)

Rate +0.020%, long 0.1 BTC, mark 45,000: payment = 0.1 × 45,000 × 0.0002 = 0.9 XRP, paid by the long to the short.

5. Trading fees

Calculation EXISTING

fee = amount × price × rate
rate = maker rate if the order rested, taker rate if it crossed

The rate comes from the fee engine: volume tiers per account, with a per-market override (CalculateTradingFee, perpetuals_service.go:3845-3878). The config default is maker 0.02%, taker 0.06%, and the fee-engine perp tiers start at 3.5 bps taker and fall with volume. The fee is computed in the market's quote asset, so for XRP it is already in XRP.

Rules for the XRP markets NEW

  • Rate level. Same bps ladder as the same-base USDT market, so a user sees one fee schedule. The fee is debited in XRP from the position's XRP margin or the XRP cross balance.
  • Reserve at order time. Initial margin plus taker fee on notional, both in XRP, are locked when the order is placed.
  • Volume for tiering and campaigns. USD volume = XRP notional × mark(XRPUSDT) at trade time. Today TradeVolumeUSDT takes the quote notional as is, which would undercount XRP volume by the XRP price (section 06).
  • USD value of the fee. fee_XRP × mark(XRPUSDT). Today it comes out as 0 for XRP (section 06).
  • Rebates and referral payouts. TODO: pay in XRP, or convert to USDT through XRPUSDT spot. The second needs the XRPUSDT spot market to be live.
  • Liquidation fee. liquidation_fee_rate × liquidated notional, in XRP, credited to the insurance pool of the base asset. TODO: confirm the exact path in the liquidation code before implementation. Venues differ: Binance charges a clearance fee into the insurance fund, OKX charges the taker rate, Bybit and Hyperliquid charge none (section 6).
  • Fee currency precedent. Binance COIN-M and OKX coin-margined contracts charge the fee in the margin coin, which is the same shape as an XRP fee.
  • Funding payments carry no fee.

Liquidation price (isolated long, approximate, fees ignored)

liq_price_long  ≈ entry × (1 − 1/leverage + MMR)
liq_price_short ≈ entry × (1 + 1/leverage − MMR)
Worked example (illustrative)

A taker buys 0.1 BTC at 45,000 at 10x. Notional 4,500 XRP; initial margin 450 XRP; taker fee at 6 bps = 2.7 XRP; maker fee at 2 bps would be 0.9 XRP. With XRPUSDT at 2.00 the taker fee is worth 5.4 USD and the trade counts as 9,000 USD of volume.

What this means for our rules

  • Funding in the market is universally a premium index plus a clamped interest term, settled on an 8 h grid or shorter. Our phase 1 leg difference is not an industry formula; it is chosen because no book exists yet and because it equals the maker's hedge carry. Phase 2 moves to the standard formula.
  • Mark is universally index plus a smoothed basis, usually a median of three. A pure ratio is a deliberate phase 1 simplification.
  • Mark, not index, is the price used for the funding payment on most venues (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, Gate). dYdX and Hyperliquid use the oracle. We keep mark.
  • Fee currency follows the margin coin on coin-margined contracts. No venue lists an XRP-quoted perpetual, so every XRP-specific value (cap, margin rate, fee level) is our own risk decision.
  • Dynamic funding intervals (1 h to 8 h) exist on Binance, OKX, Bybit and Gate to limit shocks. Worth adding in phase 2.

07Pools, Risk Controls and LaunchUPDATED v6

The market maker carries the directional risk. What remains for the platform is liquidation shortfall, in XRP, on the settlement pool. Pools are partitioned by settlement asset (the multiple-settlement-assets design §8.3, §8.4); below is the re-examined picture.

1. How the pools work today

There are three settlement and three insurance system accounts; the pool is chosen by base asset (BTC and ETH are pool 1). Balances are one row per (account, asset), and a row is created the first time an asset is booked (getOrCreatePerpMargin in the FSM). So BTCXRP PnL lands on the XRP row of settlement pool 1 and never touches the USDT row.

3. Where a loss goes

  1. User marginAn isolated position can lose at most its allocated margin. A cross position can lose at most the XRP cross group's balance; USDT balances are not touched. User accounts cannot go negative from a trade (margin_manager/settlement.go:243-275).
  2. Insurance pool (XRP row)Starts at 0 and is clamped at 0. It grows only from liquidation fees and surplus.
  3. Settlement pool (XRP row)Absorbs what insurance cannot cover and can go negative ("unfunded absorbed by settlement", raft/fsm/balance.go:1008-1035). Accepted losses sit here.
  4. ADLWhether and when ADL triggers while the settlement pool is negative was not verified; it is a release-gate item (§12 of that design: "Missing/zero XRP insurance balance follows the documented safe path").
The loss is in XRP

A negative XRP balance is a liability whose USD value rises with XRP. Finance must decide whether to top it up periodically or let it accumulate.

Open-interest cap per marketIn quote-asset terms. Above the cap only reduce-only orders are accepted. B-Book has no such limit today.

Conservative parameters10x max leverage. Maintenance margin above the same-base USDT market.
Parameters in XRP unitsRisk tiers, min_notional, tick and precision per market. The default min_notional=1 means 1 XRP.

  1. Steps 1 to 6 of the settlement-assets designXRP starts as transfer_only; verify the spot → perp → spot round trip.
  2. Create the markets frozenoperational_state=FROZEN, visibility INVISIBLE. The derived market starts publishing; check the mark, index and funding events and the 05a guards.
  3. Run the §12 release gates of that designIncluding USDT and XRP cross positions in one account not affecting each other, and XRP liquidation using XRP pools only.
  4. Enable XRP; unfreeze for the market maker onlyThe market is unfrozen but stays INVISIBLE, so only market_maker_sessions can trade. The market maker deposits XRP and rests two-sided orders.
  5. DrillsWith test accounts, run one funding settlement, one isolated liquidation and one XRP cross liquidation. Check pool balances and alerts.
  6. Open to usersOpen one market first; open the second after a period of observation.
Confirmed

FROZEN rejects every order, including the market maker's. So the "demonstrate while frozen" part of that design is done in steps 2 and 3, and market-maker quoting and drills come after the step 4 unfreeze, with the market still INVISIBLE. 08Limitations and Open QuestionsUPDATED v6

What phase 1 does not do, what it depends on, and which questions need an answer.

Limitations

  • NEVERThese markets cannot move to A-Book. No external venue lists the contracts (checked 2026-11-18), and mm_connector needs a Binance symbol.
  • MMLiquidity depends entirely on the internal market maker. If it stops, the market goes reduce-only.
  • DEPENDSLaunch depends on the multiple-settlement-assets design being complete and passing its release gates (§12).
  • LOSSBad debt is in XRP and lands on the settlement pool, because the insurance pool starts at 0.
  • DEPENDSXRPUSDT-PERP must keep running: the synthetic price, the funding rate and USD conversion all use it. Both legs use only Binance in production; a Binance outage puts the derived market into SAFE_MODE.
  • DEPENDSAn XRPUSDT spot market must be listed: referral conversion and analytics pricing need it.
