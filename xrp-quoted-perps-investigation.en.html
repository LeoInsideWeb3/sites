XRP-Quoted B-Book Perpetuals: Feasibility and Design

Feasible, medium risk. Matching, settlement and funding transfers already work per quote asset. The account side, meaning margin and liquidation grouped by settlement asset, XRP transfers, the account-summary API and pool routing, follows the multiple-settlement-assets design (Perpetuals: Multiple Settlement Assets, status Proposed) (chapter 04). This proposal covers only what that design does not: the index and mark price (our own last traded price), the funding rate (leg difference, decided 2026-11-26), USD reporting values, B-Book risk caps and the launch configuration.

Main risks

Self-referential price (accepted)The mark is our own last trade, so one fill can move it and trigger liquidations. The requester accepted this on 2026-11-25. Our own market maker guarantees depth and keeps the price close to BTCUSDT / XRPUSDT; an alert watches the gap (07-4). Liquidation and ADL executions also reach the mark (05c-4).
Bad debt is in XRPThe XRP insurance pool starts at 0 (a confirmed assumption, chapter 02), so every liquidation shortfall lands on the settlement pool as a negative XRP balance. Its USD value grows when XRP rises.
Funding does not always net to zeroB-Book funding debits and credits each position separately with no counter account; a skipped or failed leg creates or destroys XRP. Phase 1 measures it per interval; phase 2 routes funding through a clearing account (05a-4).
Liquidity depends on one market makerThese markets can never move to A-Book. If the internal MM stops quoting, the market can only go reduce-only.
Higher volatilityA cross pair moves with both BTC and XRP. Leverage must be lower and maintenance margin higher than on USDT markets.

02Product Definition

The request can be read in two ways. This document follows Definition A, as confirmed by the requester.

A. XRP-quoted, XRP-margined (chosen)B. USDT market, XRP as collateral

Engine changeSmall: reuses "quote asset is the collateral"Large: haircuts and collateral liquidation

Confirmed assumptions

  • Markets are XRP-quoted and XRP-margined. PnL and fees are paid in XRP.
  • Liquidity and hedging are provided by our own market maker. The MM is an ordinary perp account that rests orders through trading-api and hedges outside this repository.
  • The insurance pool starts with 0 XRP. Losses are accepted on the pool accounts.
  • The design follows the practice of the largest centralised exchanges where one applies.

What users should understand

A long BTCXRP position gains when BTC rises against XRP. The gain is paid in XRP, so its USD value also depends on the XRP price. A trader who is right on BTC/XRP can still see a smaller USD result if XRP falls against the dollar. The UI should show both the XRP amount and an indicative USD value.

Why most exchanges do not list this

A check of public instrument lists on 2026-11-18 found no BTC/XRP or ETH/XRP perpetual, and no XRP-quoted market of any kind, on Binance, OKX, Bybit, Kraken, Coinbase, Bitget, Gate or Deribit (details in 05b). A market maker has to hedge with two legs (BTCUSDT and XRPUSDT), the hedge earns USDT while the liability is XRP, and demand is spread thin. The design below accepts these costs because we have a committed internal market maker.

Closest precedent: Binance ETHBTC perpetual

Binance lists one perpetual with a non-stablecoin quote: ETHBTC, quoted and margined in BTC, live since May 2023. It is the same shape as Definition A, and its index and funding setup is the reference for this design.

3. Hedge ratio: how the market maker replicates the contract

Long q BTCXRP equals opposite positions of equal notional on the two USDT legs, not equal coin amounts. Below, B = mark(BTCUSDT), X = mark(XRPUSDT), S = B / X.

LegSideAmountUSDT notional
BTCUSDTLongq BTCq × B
XRPUSDTShortq × S XRPq × S × X = q × B

Short BTCXRP reverses both legs.

# prices move from (B0, X0) to (B1, X1)
hedge_pnl_USDT = q × (B1 − B0) − q × S0 × (X1 − X0)
               = q × X1 × (S1 − S0)
contract_pnl_XRP = q × (S1 − S0)      # equals the line above once converted at X1

A hedge built at the current S is exact at that moment. The currencies differ, though: the hedge PnL lands in USDT while the market maker's PnL on our market is in XRP. After prices move, the XRP short must be brought back to q × S, or the USDT PnL converted to XRP; otherwise the accumulated PnL is exposed to XRP/USDT. Under funding option B (05a-4) the leg difference is exact only at this ratio.

  • The rebalancing threshold (how far S may drift before an adjustment) is the market maker's call (TODO). Hedging is outside this repo; we only state the requirement in the market-maker integration notes.
  • Residual risk: our price is our own last trade, not B / X, so any gap between it and S is basis between our market and the hedge. Keeping the two close is part of the market maker's commitment. The market maker bears it.
  • Fees and funding are paid in XRP; the market maker decides whether to convert back to USDT or hold XRP inventory.

What this means for our rules

  • Funding in the market is universally a premium index plus a clamped interest term, settled on an 8 h grid or shorter.   • Mark, not index, is the price used for the funding payment on most venues (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, Gate). dYdX and Hyperliquid use the oracle. We keep mark.
  • Fee currency follows the margin coin on coin-margined contracts. No venue lists an XRP-quoted perpetual, so every XRP-specific value (cap, margin rate, fee level) is our own risk decision.
  • Dynamic funding intervals (1 h to 8 h) exist on Binance, OKX, Bybit and Gate to limit shocks. Worth adding in phase 2.
